# Code of conduct

SAGE is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically constructive community.

Participants are expected to communicate with empathy, critique ideas and code rather than
people, welcome different backgrounds and levels of experience, respect privacy, and accept
maintainer decisions made to protect the project and its users. Harassment, discriminatory or
demeaning language, threats, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and
sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or other participation that violates
these expectations and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed.
Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid unnecessary
disclosure about affected people.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's
[security advisory form](https://github.com/NeuralBinary/SAGE/security/advisories/new), marking
the report as a code-of-conduct concern. Do not include sensitive personal information beyond
what is needed to understand and address the incident. Retaliation against a reporter or
participant in an investigation is prohibited.
