# SAGE Commercial License

## Why Buy a Commercial License?

The AGPL-3.0 license is great for open source projects, research, and community use. But if you're building a proprietary product or SaaS offering, the AGPL's source-disclosure requirements may not work for you.

A commercial license removes those requirements and gives you:

- **No open source disclosure** — Use SAGE in closed-source products without sharing your modifications.
- **No AGPL compliance overhead** — Focus on your product, not license audits.
- **Enterprise-friendly terms** — Indemnification options available.
- **Priority support** — Get help when you need it.
- **Custom feature development** — We'll build what you need.

## Who Needs a Commercial License?

- Companies embedding SAGE into proprietary software
- SaaS providers offering SAGE as a managed service
- Organizations that can't open-source their modifications to SAGE
- Enterprises with strict legal/compliance requirements

## Who Doesn't Need a Commercial License?

- Open source projects (AGPL-3.0 covers you completely)
- Internal evaluation and testing (AGPL-3.0 covers this too)
- Academic and non-commercial research (AGPL-3.0 covers this)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I use SAGE for free if I'm an open source project?**
A: Yes. AGPL-3.0 covers open source use completely.

**Q: What if I'm a non-profit or academic institution?**
A: Contact us—we offer special terms.

**Q: Do I need a commercial license if I'm just testing SAGE internally?**
A: No. Internal evaluation and testing are covered under AGPL. You only need a commercial license when you ship a product or service to users.

**Q: How is this different from just using the AGPL?**
A: AGPL requires you to share your source code if you modify SAGE and run it as a network service. A commercial license waives that requirement.

**Q: What if I want to contribute to SAGE while using it commercially?**
A: Contributions are welcome under our Contributor License Agreement (see CONTRIBUTING.md). Your contributions remain open source while your commercial use is covered by your license.

**Q: Do you offer source-code escrow?**
A: Yes, for enterprise customers who require it.
