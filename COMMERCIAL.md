SAGE source on `main`, and releases v0.2.7 and later, are available under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later (`AGPL-3.0-or-later`). NeuralBinary may also offer a separate commercial license as an alternative set of terms.

This page is a product and licensing overview, not the commercial license agreement itself and not legal advice. The rights and obligations for any commercial customer are defined only by the signed commercial agreement.

## When to consider a commercial license

A commercial license may be useful when an organization wants to use, modify, distribute, embed, or operate SAGE under terms different from the AGPL. Common situations include proprietary products, closed-source services, OEM distribution, or internal legal/compliance policies that do not permit AGPL-licensed dependencies.

The AGPL is an open-source license and does not prohibit commercial use. Whether a particular deployment triggers source-availability or other AGPL obligations depends on the facts of that deployment. Organizations should evaluate those obligations with their own counsel.

## Commercial options

Commercial terms are quoted based on the use case and may be structured for:

- **Startup use** — early-stage organizations and limited deployments.
- **Enterprise use** — larger deployments, support requirements, or negotiated legal terms.
- **OEM / embedded use** — distribution of SAGE as part of another product.
- **Hosted-service use** — deployments where alternative terms are preferred for a proprietary service.

Support, service levels, indemnification, source-code escrow, custom development, and other enterprise terms are available only when they are expressly included in the signed agreement.

## Request commercial terms

Contact **sage@digitalacre.org** and include:

1. Organization name and intended use case.
2. Whether SAGE will be distributed, embedded, or operated as a service.
3. Expected deployment scale.
4. Any support, SLA, security, procurement, or custom-development requirements.

## Open-source use

You do not need a commercial license merely because your use is commercial. You may use SAGE under `AGPL-3.0-or-later` whenever you are willing and able to comply with that license. See [LICENSE](LICENSE) for the open-source terms.
